### 4. Synthetic Data & Ethical Research Statement
- All entities, accounts, card numbers, transaction traces, conversational scripts, and media artifacts generated by FORGE are **entirely synthetic and non-routable**.
- FORGE operates under a strict defensive charter: red-team strategies explore hypothetical attack topologies to discover defender blind spots and train ML classifiers; they are never executed against live systems, production payment rails, or real individuals.
